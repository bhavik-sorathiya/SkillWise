# SkillWise: AI-Powered Career Development

Welcome to **SkillWise**! 

SkillWise is a next-generation platform designed to help job seekers and professionals prepare for the modern hiring landscape. By combining artificial intelligence with real-time feedback, SkillWise takes the guesswork out of resume building and interview preparation.

## What is SkillWise?

At its core, SkillWise is your personal career coach. We know that landing a dream job requires two things: an outstanding resume that gets past the screening filters, and the confidence to nail the interview. SkillWise helps you master both.

### Core Features

*   **Intelligent Resume Analysis**: Upload your resume (PDF or Word doc), and our AI engine instantly analyzes it. We identify missing keywords, suggest formatting improvements, and highlight areas where you can showcase your impact more effectively.
*   **Real-Time Mock Interviews**: Practice makes perfect. Engage in interactive, real-time mock interviews customized to your industry. The system listens, analyzes your responses, and provides constructive feedback on your communication style and technical accuracy.
*   **Skill Tracking & Growth**: Keep track of the skills you have and the skills you need. SkillWise helps you visualize your professional growth over time, ensuring you are always competitive in the job market.
*   **Seamless User Experience**: A beautiful, responsive interface that works flawlessly across all your devices, allowing you to prepare for your career anytime, anywhere.

## Why We Built This

The hiring process is increasingly automated and competitive. Many great candidates are filtered out simply because their resumes don't hit the right keywords, or because they lack the opportunity to practice their interviewing skills in a low-stakes environment. 

We built SkillWise to democratize access to high-quality career coaching. By leveraging advanced AI, we provide personalized, actionable feedback that was previously only available through expensive professional services.
